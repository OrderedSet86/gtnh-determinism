# gtnh-determinism 0.15.0

Since `abd1aa7c3ef77b062c9b5bd89a89e86ae4268ec8` (0.14.0).

## Summary

- **GTNH 2.9.0 support, including the new default world type.** 2.9.0 ships Realistic-World-Gen-2
  (RWG2), and new singleplayer worlds default to `RWG2_CONTINENTS`, which has oceans between continents.
  The RWG fixes now cover RWG2 too.
- **RWG decoration was never fixed before this release.** In 0.14.0 and earlier, the method name in two RWG
  mixins does not exist in the shipped RWG jar, so those mixins failed silently. One half of the big-tree
  fix did apply, and with only that half, no no-argument big oak (Wood Hills, Wood Mountains) generated.
  Both mixins now bind on 2.8.4 and 2.9.0, and big oaks generate again.
- **RWG ores and decoration are a function of seed and position.** RWG and RWG2 reseed their populate
  `Random` before the ore pass and before decoration, and each decorator call gets a `Random` seeded
  from the call's position.
- **This release changes worldgen** on 2.8.4 and 2.9.0 `rwg` worlds. A world, seed list or route map made
  with 0.14.0 does not match a world made with 0.15.0.
- **The route map asks for the world type.** The preselected world type is the instance's default.
- **Supported packs are 2.8.4 and 2.9.0.** 2.7.4, the 2.9.0 betas and the RCs are not tested with this release. On
  GregTech 5.09.52.31 to 5.09.54.01 (2.9.0-beta-1), the jar logs a warning and leaves ore veins unfixed.
- **Tested on** 2.8.4 and 2.9.0 (both world types): four seeds each, `rows` vs `spiral` at radius 8, plus
  one launch pair, and six more `RWG2_CONTINENTS` seeds with Witchery structures near spawn. Dungeon rooms and chest contents were identical in every pair with the default settings.
- **Known limits:** whole trees, Et Futurum deepslate ore host stone, deepslate↔gravel at the bottom of
  the world and GregTech stone-layer blobs still differ between walk orders: 0 to 16,187 blocks per seed at
  radius 8.

The sections below have the detail and the measurements.

## What changes in a world

The reference-seed route map (`-1636594104014467454`, 2.9.0-beta-3, `rwg`, overworld at radius 120) was
rebuilt with this build and compared with the 0.14.0 build of the same map:

| comparison | chests identical | only in 0.14.0 | only in 0.15.0 | contents changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.14.0 vs 0.15.0 with `-Dgtnhdet.rwgdecoseed=false` | 6,271 of 6,271 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 0.14.0 vs 0.15.0 | 6,258 of 6,271 | 11 | 12 | 2 |

So every chest difference comes from the new RWG seeding, not from the big-oak fix. The seeding moves
RWG ore blobs and decoration. Those blocks are part of the live walls and floors that vanilla dungeon
chests, mineshaft minecarts and village houses are placed against. The 25 changed containers are 9 vanilla
dungeon and mineshaft chests, 9 chests found only by full generation, 2 Forestry apiaries, 2 Witchery
containers, 1 village chest and 2 mineshaft minecarts with new positions. One village house is gone: the
TinkerHouse chest at x 176, z -490. That chest is still in the stage-0 prediction, so 2,393 of the 2,394 predicted
chests are present (0.14.0: 2,394). The vanilla dungeon count goes from 1,224 to 1,225. Ore veins differ
only at the window edge, the same as between two 0.14.0-era builds.

Big oaks generate again in Wood Hills and Wood Mountains on every RWG world.

## Fixed

### RWG decoration and big oaks: the fixes never bound

Mod jars are reobfuscated, so an override of `WorldGenerator.generate` exists only as `func_76484_a` in
RWG alpha-1.5.0 (2.8.4), alpha-1.5.3 (2.9.0) and RWG2 2.0.6. `RwgDecoForkMixin` and the `generate` half of
`DecoBigTreeCtorMixin` named `generate` with `remap = false`, so they matched nothing in production. The
late mixin config has no `defaultRequire`, so neither failed. The constructor half of
`DecoBigTreeCtorMixin` did bind and set `size = -1` on every no-argument `DecoBigTree`.
`validTreeLocation()` then rejected every such tree.

Every RWG and RWG2 injector now names `{ "func_76484_a", "generate" }` and has `require = 1`, so a miss
stops the game at startup. Class dumps (`-Dmixin.debug.export=true`) on 2.8.4 and 2.9.0 show each handler
called from `func_76484_a`.

### RWG populate stream

`ChunkGeneratorRealistic.populate` draws structure pieces, lakes, dungeons, ores and decoration from one
per-chunk `Random`. Lakes, village houses and many decorators return before their draws when the live
terrain fails a test, so each later stage of a chunk read a stream position that depended on what was
already generated. Two changes remove that dependence, for RWG and RWG2:

- `RwgPopulateReseedMixin` and `Rwg2PopulateReseedMixin` reseed the populate `Random` from (world seed,
  chunk, stage) when `populate` builds `OreGenEvent.Pre` and `DecorateBiomeEvent.Pre`.
- `RwgDecoForkMixin` and `Rwg2DecoForkMixin` wrap each of the 29 decorators and pass a `Random` seeded from
  (world seed, x, y, z, decorator class). A decorator draws nothing from the shared stream.

`-Dgtnhdet.rwgdecoseed=false` turns both off.

### RWG2's Witchery foundation check read the live world

RWG2 vetoes a Witchery structure when the ground under the structure is uneven or unsupported
(`WitcheryWorldgenCompat.hasUnsuitableFoundation`). `hasUnsuitableFoundation` reads the top block and the blocks below
the top block across the structure's footprint. The footprint crosses chunk borders, so whether the structure
generated depended on which neighbouring chunks were already populated. `Rwg2WitcheryFoundationMixin`
sends both reads to virgin terrain. `-Dgtnhdet.rwg2witchery=false` restores the live reads. Reported in
PR 4.

Measured on six seeds with a Witchery structure within 6 chunks of spawn, `rows` vs `spiral` at radius 8.
With the live reads, the probe generated 745 chunks on the `rows` walk of seed 6085785342464883720 and 750 on `spiral`, and populated 607 and 615. The extra chunks around x 0, z -224 differ by 43,086 blocks, and one of those chunks has a
chest that `rows` lacks. With virgin reads, both walks have 750 chunks generated and 615 populated, the chests are identical, and 98 blocks differ. On the other five seeds the chests are identical, and the chunk counts
are the same with and without the fix.

### RWG and RWG2 kept the first world's noise setting

Both mods cache the overworld's `rwg_data`, the saved noise implementation, in a static field on
first use and never reload the field. A world opened later in the same game session generated with the
first world's noise setting. Terrain differs only if a world earlier in the session had `/rwg_noise` set
to an OpenSimplex mode. `RwgWorldSavedDataMixin` and `Rwg2WorldSavedDataMixin` drop the cache when the
overworld changes. Reported for RWG2 in PR 4.

### Dungeon attempt counters

The per-chunk dungeon attempt counters are removed when `populate` returns. Before this release, the counters had one entry for
each chunk generated, for as long as the game ran.

## Added

### RWG2 coverage

Realistic-World-Gen-2 2.0.6 (mod id `rwg2`) is a separate mod with copies of RWG's generator and
decorators under `rwg2.*`. `Rwg2DecoForkMixin`, `Rwg2DecoBigTreeCtorMixin`, `Rwg2DungeonAttemptMixin`,
`Rwg2PopulateReseedMixin`, `Rwg2WorldSavedDataMixin` and, with Witchery, `Rwg2WitcheryFoundationMixin`
apply when `rwg2` is loaded. The dungeon-attempt logic is shared with RWG in
`RwgDungeonAttempts`.

### Route map: world type

`routemap.py` reads the default world type and the allowed list from the instance's
`config/defaultworldgenerator.cfg`. The window has a World type list. At the console, the player is asked
when the instance allows more than one type. On the command line, `--level-type` sets the world type. `make-routemap.py` passes the
world type to the server and to the stage-0 prefilter, and writes the world type as `level_type` to `meta.json`. A
finished map is reused only for the same pack and world type. Maps from earlier releases count as `RWG`.

### Probe and seed search

- `-Dprobe.worldtype` selects the prefilter's world type (default `rwg`). In the probe scripts,
  `level-type` is `RWG2_CONTINENTS` when RWG2 is in `mods/`, and `rwg` otherwise. `LEVEL_TYPE` overrides the `level-type`.
- `biomes.json` covers biome ids above 255 (RWG2 uses 300 to 329) and records `worldType` and RWG's
  `rwgBiomeListVersion`.
- The meteor analytic screen stops with an error on any world type but `rwg`. The screen's thresholds
  were calibrated on `rwg` terrain.

### Java 25

The fix jar and the probe jar build with Java 25 through JvmDowngrader (`enableModernJavaSyntax =
jvmDowngrader`). The jars still run on the pack's Java 8 to 25 runtimes. JvmDowngrader's runtime stubs are
shaded into each jar, under `gtnhdeterminism/xyz/wagyourtail/jvmdg/` in the fix jar and
`worldgenprobe/xyz/wagyourtail/jvmdg/` in the probe jar, because 2.8.4 ships no copy. The stubs are
LGPL-2.1. Each jar has JvmDowngrader's license and a notice for the stubs in `licenses/jvmdowngrader/`.

## Removed

- The GregTech 5.09.52.31 to 5.09.54.01 ore implementation: `WorldgenGTOreLayerFindStoneTypeMixin`,
  `OreManagerDryRunSuppressMixin`, `GTWorldGenContainerDryRunMixin`, `OreManagerVirginStoneB1Mixin`,
  `OreManagerVirginStoneB2Mixin`, `GtOreFusedWorldgen` and `VirginStoneType.atUngated`. Of the GTNH
  packs, only 2.9.0-beta-1 has a GregTech version in that band.

## Known limits

- **Trees and deep blocks still depend on walk order.** Differing blocks, `rows` vs `spiral` at radius 8,
  fix jar:

  | pack, world type | -777 | 42 | 1234567890 | 8675309 | launch pair, -777 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 2.9.0, `RWG2_CONTINENTS` | 4 | 66 | 1 | 12 | 4 |
  | 2.9.0, `rwg` | 133 | 0 | 839 | 172 | 23 |
  | 2.8.4, `rwg` | 1 | 2 | 72 | 0 | 1 |

  The `RWG2_CONTINENTS` row is this release's jar on RWG2 2.0.6. The `rwg` rows are an earlier build of
  this release, before the Witchery, saved-data and Java 25 changes, on the 2.9.0 pack with RWG2 2.0.3.
  On `rwg`, those changes only affect a second world opened in the same game session.

  The blocks are whole trees present on one walk only (vanilla, Thaumcraft great and silverwood trees,
  BiomesOPlenty leaves written over vanilla ones), `etfuturum:deepslate_redstone_ore` ↔
  `etfuturum:deepslate`, and `deepslate` ↔ `gravel` at y 1 to 4. The -777 launch pairs differ by the same
  blocks as the -777 walk pairs: 4 blocks of one tree on `RWG2_CONTINENTS`, and 23 blocks of Et Futurum glow
  lichen, cave vines and deepslate↔gravel on `rwg`. With jar 0.14.0, 2.9.0 `rwg` seed -777 differs by 679 blocks, against 133 here.

  On the six Witchery seeds (`RWG2_CONTINENTS`, virgin reads) the walk pairs differ by 0, 11, 57, 98, 214 and
  16,187 blocks. Seed 8218033039576139462 has the 16,187: 11,759 blocks of a GregTech red granite blob
  around x -113, z 63 that only the `rows` walk generated, and dirt and gravel pockets, a Thaumcraft tree
  and BiomesOPlenty mud around x -145, z 114 that only the `spiral` walk generated. The live-read arm of
  that seed differs by 16,125 blocks in the same places.
- **A village chest in the stage-0 prediction is absent** on the reference seed: the TinkerHouse at x 176, z -490.

- **`seedsearch/rwg-analytic` does not predict RWG2 terrain.** `seedsearch/rwg-analytic` is a port of RWG terrain. Seed searches
  on `RWG2_CONTINENTS` must use the full probe.
- **Stage-0 prefilter predictions on `RWG2_CONTINENTS` are checked for spawn only.** Spawn landed in the
  same chunk as full generation on two seeds.
- **RWG and RWG2 both save world data as `rwg_data`, and both register `/rwg_noise` and `/rwg_buginfo`.**
  Worldgen is unaffected. Reading one mod's data on the other mod's world type throws
  `ClassCastException`. To report upstream.

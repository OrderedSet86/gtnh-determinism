# gtnh-determinism 0.12.0

Since `c5c27e7d97fa8afcbab492a60a102d97f89180fd` (0.11.0).

Everything here is chest and structure **placement**. It is not a claim about blocks — see
*Known limits*.

## Fixed

### Village chests were filled the wrong number of times (F10b, regression from 0.11)

0.11 added a batch rebuild so a chest filled more than once keeps every batch. Its fill counter used
a single last-seen inventory, which assumed repeat fills are consecutive on the thread. For Village
Names pieces they are not: `MapGenStructure.generateStructuresInChunk` calls `addComponentParts` once
per `[16k+8 .. 16k+23]` clip box intersecting the piece, and those pieces clip only their block
placement, not the fill — so one chest is refilled by several invocations with other chests in
between. The counter read `0,0,0,1` where it intended `0,1,2,3`.

The count is now taken **per site scope**, in a map on the `Site` frame rather than a thread-wide
slot. That yields vanilla's number: `StructureComponent.generateStructureChestContents` is guarded by
both `isVecInside` and `getBlock != Blocks.chest`, so vanilla fills a structure chest exactly once
however many chunk boxes intersect it. Generators that genuinely fill one inventory twice in a single
pass still accumulate — `WorldGenHilltopStones` has two deliberate call sites and still gets two
batches.

**Balance:** the 11 multi-fill Village Names sites drop from **58 to 34 stacks**. Hilltop circles are
unchanged at 249 stacks across 20 sites, site for site.

### Vanilla dungeon chest placement was route-dependent

`WorldGenDungeons` picks each chest position with two draws off the shared populate `Random`, whose
position on arrival depends on how many mossy-cobblestone rolls the wall loop took — one per solid
block, so a function of the surrounding terrain. Those two draws (`nextInt` ordinals **3 and 4**; the
wall loop's `nextInt(4)` is ordinal 2) now come from a per-room splitmix fork, indexed by a counter
keyed per room anchor and reset at `generate` HEAD.

The chest loop's own block reads — the candidate cell and its four adjacency tests — are answered
from the room's own model (virgin terrain plus the room's geometry, extents re-derived from the same
fork that built it) instead of the live world. Placement is therefore a pure function of
(seed, room), with **no neighbouring-room lookups and no cross-chunk reads**, which keeps it
computable by a worldless prefilter.

**Balance:** positions are redistributed, quantity is not. 59 chests against 58 and 409 stacks
against 410 at radius 20.

### Overlapping dungeon rooms no longer destroy each other's loot

Rooms overlap at about 0.7 pairs per 100 rooms, and that rate is stock — measured with the scan lever
off, 15 pairs against 15. What was not stock was the outcome: the second room's interior carve went
straight through the first room's chest, and which room was second depended on chunk population
order. The carve and the wall placement now step around chests and spawners. The rooms still merge
into one continuous space; both keep their contents.

**Balance:** +3 chests preserved per 25 seeds that stock destroyed.

`-Dgtnhdet.dungeonchest`, `-Dgtnhdet.dungeonmerge`.

## Known limits

Read these before describing the release as "deterministic worldgen".

- **Blocks are roughly 75% route-dependent.** 25 seeds, both walk orders, block hashes on: 72-79% of
  chunks differ, against a same-order floor of 0.1-0.5%. Noise is uniform through y 0-95, so it is
  not any single structure. Untouched by this release. `warm-shard.sh` defaults `PROBE_NOHASH=true`,
  which is why this went unmeasured for so long.
- **Route-dependent lakes demolish finished structures.** 12 dungeon chests still differ between walk
  orders; 10 of them are filled in *both* arms and then deleted by a BoP poison lake (62 blocks in one
  order against 3 in the other). Nothing inside `WorldGenDungeons` can close this.
- **Thaumcraft greatwood tree chests are worse than dungeons now**: 17 against 47 across the two walk
  orders, spiral >= rows on all 25 seeds. A monotone bias, so not RNG skew and not fixable with a
  fork. `BiomeGenMagicalForest` supplies greatwood through vanilla `BiomeDecorator` off the shared
  decoration stream.
- **Minecart chests are invisible to every measurement.** `buildSearchReport` walks tile entities, so
  all 156 `EntityMinecartChest` at radius 60 are absent from every corpus, loot CSV and map layer —
  about 930 stacks of `mineshaftCorridor`, roughly 23% of the vanilla-dungeon loot volume. Routemap
  chest coverage is therefore capped near 92% of what actually generates.
- `gtnhdet$forkedExtentX/Z` do not consume from the shared `rand`, so populate takes 2 fewer draws per
  dungeon attempt than stock. Deterministic, but the javadoc's claim that populate's draw count is
  unchanged is false. Correcting it moves worldgen against stock and needs its own A/B.

## Also

- `scripts/diff-dungeons.py` (new): separates dungeon **room existence** from **chest placement**,
  which a position-keyed chest diff conflates. Excludes the warm boot world, bounds the comparison to
  chunks both walks attempted in, and refuses to print a verdict over zero seeds. Note it counts what
  the generator *filled*, not what the world *holds*.
- `probe-build` `chest-sites.json` reverted to no `fills` column; `VillageChestPrefilter` rolls a
  single batch again, matching vanilla.
- `[chesttrace]` now carries `fillidx=`, so a repeat fill is visible directly instead of having to be
  reconstructed from cumulative roll totals.
